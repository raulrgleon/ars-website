Recycling

On most residential routes for more than 25 years. Call to check yours.

Sort into three groups

Set everything at the curb by 7 a.m. on pickup day, with a little space between recycling and trash.

Do not mix the first two groups. Mixed loads go in the trash truck.

Not sure about an item? Throw it out, or call 800-638-8492.

Plastic, tin and aluminum

  • Plastic marked 1 or 2: bottles, jugs, pill bottles
  • Rinse it, toss the cap, leave the label
  • Tin and aluminum cans, rinsed or emptied
  • No plastic grocery bags
  • Use a bin or clear bag

Paper and cardboard

  • Clean, dry paper, magazines, mail and boxes
  • Flatten boxes and remove plastic or wax liners
  • Bundle it or use a paper sack or box
  • Keep it indoors if rain or snow is coming

Trash

  • Bags or cans up to 40 gallons
  • Nothing over 50 pounds
  • Businesses: call to ask about commercial recycling
